Privacy, cookies and terms

Last updated: October 2026.

What we store

  • Your Google account ID (a number Google gives us). We do not store your email address, name or Google photo, and we never see your Google password.
  • The username and age you choose. Both are shown publicly next to your stories and replies.
  • Your profile photo, if you add one. It is shrunk to a tiny picture and shown publicly next to your posts.
  • Your stories, replies and broken hearts.
  • One sign-in cookie so the site knows you are signed in. It is needed for the site to work.

Public content

Everything you post is public and can be read by anyone without an account. Do not write anything you want to keep private, such as phone numbers, addresses or other people's full names. You can delete your own stories and replies at any time. Deleting a story also deletes its replies and broken hearts.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Ads use cookies and similar technology, and Google may use them to show ads based on your visits. We only load ads after you choose "Allow ads". If you choose "No ads", no advertising cookies are set. You can clear your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who can use this site

You need a Google account to post, reply or break a heart. People of any age may read. If you are young, ask a parent or guardian before sharing a story. Your age and photo are shown publicly, so think about whether you are comfortable with that. Do not use a photo of anyone else.

Rules

  • Text only. No images, GIFs or videos.
  • Share your own experience. Do not attack, threaten, harass or name and shame other people.
  • No hate, sexual content involving minors, or content that encourages harm to anyone.
  • One Google account, one username. Do not try to get around the limits with other accounts.
  • Profile photos must be of yourself or an avatar, and must not be offensive.
  • We can remove content or accounts that break these rules. Use the Report button on anything that does.

Not a crisis service

Retired Heart is a place to share and read stories. It is not counselling or emergency help. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country.
