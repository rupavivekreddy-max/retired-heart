How it works

Share the story that broke you. Let others feel it.

Retired Heart is a place for love and family stories that ended in pain. Anyone can read. Readers react with a broken heart, and the most heartbreaking stories rise to the top.

  1. Sign in with Google

    Reading needs no account. To post, break a heart or reply, sign in with Google. One Google account gets exactly one username.

  2. Pick your username and age

    Choose a username once. It cannot be changed. Your age is shown next to your story and replies.

  3. Choose a language and write

    Pick the language of your story, then write in it. Text only. No pictures, GIFs or videos.

  4. Read other stories and break hearts

    Tap the broken heart on a story that hurt you. One broken heart per person on each story or reply. You cannot break a heart on your own post.

  5. Rise with the most broken hearts

    The story with the most broken hearts comes first. Replies work the same way, so the most heartbreaking reply is shown first.

Posting more than once

Everyone starts with one story. To post again, delete your earlier story, or earn it: your newest story needs more broken hearts than the number below.

Your next storyBroken hearts needed
2ndmore than 100
3rdmore than 1,000
4thmore than 10,000
5th and after10 times the one before

Replies follow the same ladder, counted separately on each story.
